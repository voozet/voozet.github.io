Profile

I am a software engineer with a research background in applied AI. My work centres on backend systems, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and getting machine-learning components into production.

I hold an M.Sc. in Information Technology Engineering from the University of Tehran, completed postgraduate research training in transparent AI and NLP at the University of Galway, and have published a peer-reviewed paper on multimodal deep learning. I have also shipped Android applications and built XR and VR systems for Meta Quest.

Currently I am a full-stack developer at Mellat Ventures, where I own the AI platform, the main website and the infrastructure under both.
